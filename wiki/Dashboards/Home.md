# WeLive2Inspire.com wiki

WeLive2Inspire.com is a one-page static website. It
started as a personal page for the diver Stefanos
Evangelos Tzivopoulos and is now a lead page that
invites visitors to try [[Zinzino]] health products or
join as partners. Stefanos appears as the brand
ambassador.
