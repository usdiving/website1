# Site History

The requirements are kept as a running list of prompts in
`myprompts/p01.md`. Git commit messages are all "update",
so that file is the best record of intent.

1. **Personal page** (2026-05-30). A one-page site for
   [[Stefanos Evangelos Tzivopoulos]] on WeLive2Inspire.com,
   with a hero photo and a short bio.
2. **Zinzino pivot.** The purpose changed to inviting
   people to [[Zinzino]]. Added the health quiz, shop,
   partner and Zoom buttons. The big photo of Stefanos
   left the top; a smaller one went to the bio near the
   bottom.
3. **Polish.** The hero image became the BalanceTest and
   BalanceOil product photo. Stefanos got a more
   professional photo and a new title list (World and
   European Masters diving champion, celebrity athlete,
   motivational speaker, network specialist, health and
   wellness advisor). The wake-up call section with the
   symptom questions was added.
4. **Photo choice.** `Stefanos_diamond.jpg` was picked as
   his photo. See [[Site Images]].

The email gate and [[EmailJS]] hookup in the
[[Health Balance Quiz]] are not in the prompt file; they
were added along the way.
