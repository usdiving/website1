# Zinzino

Zinzino is a health company that sells products like
BalanceOil and the BalanceTest, and lets people earn money
as partners selling them. The site exists to send visitors
to Zinzino, as a customer or as a partner.

The site links to Zinzino in three ways:

- the shop at `zinzino.com/2020240904`, which looks like
  Stefanos's referral id (hero, products section, quiz
  result);
- a business presentation video on `zinzino.tv`;
- the partner sign-up, which is still a `#` placeholder,
  see [[Known Gaps]].

The [[Health Balance Quiz]] frames its results around the
omega-6 to omega-3 balance that the BalanceTest measures.
[[Stefanos Evangelos Tzivopoulos]] is the brand ambassador.
